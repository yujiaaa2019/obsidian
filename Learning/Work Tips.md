## How to speak confidently in public
1. remove auditory cuttor 
	1. no ummm
	2. no and..../so..../but...
2. remove visual cuttor
	1. don't do too many hand movements 
3. smile more

## Communicating Engineering Work Business
1. when telling an engineering perspective to a business partner, tell them a story that describe them as the hero
	1. think about the problem (what the partners are struggling with) in three levels: 
		1. external problem: revenue, cost, and other product/business related benefits 
		2. internal problem: business partner's peer pressure, their own business value 
		3. moral problem: the thing is just isn't right
2. Communicating Risk
	1. when the partner makes a decision, think about the potential future risk it might encounter, and talk about what impact it will cause to the business and goals 
3. Say it with data

<mark class="hltr-pink">listen to the objections and concerns of your business partners, try to understand better what their goals and needs are, and think deeply about how engineering work impacts those goals.</mark>
