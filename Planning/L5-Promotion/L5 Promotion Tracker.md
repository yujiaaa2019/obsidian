# L5 (Engineer 5) Promotion Progress Tracker

> **Key reminder from the leveling doc**: "It is important to not interpret these criteria as checkboxes that one can cross off when demonstrated occasionally, rather one must meet these criteria on a **sustained basis**, with minimal emphasis on expected future performance."

---

## How to Use This Tracker

For each criterion below:
1. Check the box when you can point to **sustained, concrete evidence** (not a one-off)
2. Use the **Evidence** line to note specific projects, PRs, docs, or outcomes
3. Use the **Gap** line to note what's missing or what you need to work on
4. Review monthly with your manager to calibrate
5. The *L4 reference* shows what you're already expected to do — the L5 criteria show **what changes**

---

## 1. Scope and Impact

> *L4 reference: Independently takes on medium to large features within the team and drives project goals.*

- [ ] Identifies and solves **complex problems** by driving new or improving existing solutions
- [ ] Positively impacts my **team's scope of work** (not just my own tasks)
- [ ] **Generalist**: Delivers team-level architecture and project impact; leads projects for adjacent peers; drives some larger cross-functional projects (department/Director level)
- [ ] **Specialist**: Considered an expert in a technical area; directly solves technical problems within my scope
  - **Evidence**:
  - **Gap**:

> **The shift**: L4 = independent execution within your team. L5 = **identifying problems yourself** and driving solutions that impact the broader team scope. You shift from taking on work to defining what work needs to happen.

---

## 2. Technical Depth and Execution

> *L4 reference: Expands knowledge of scope enabling accurate solutions. Executes changes within team systems. Improves code touched, leverages paved road patterns, designs for extensibility/maintainability/testability. Leaves codebase in better shape.*

- [ ] **Deepens knowledge** of my scope enabling timely, accurate, and **sustainable** implementation
- [ ] Uses expertise to **develop new or extend existing capabilities**
- [ ] Significantly **improves efficiency** of team systems
- [ ] Performs **strategic migrations**, deprecations, or decommissions systems
- [ ] Solves **technical problems critical to the team**
- [ ] **Reduces complexity** in team solutions
- [ ] **Selflessly avoids local simplifications** that export complexity to other teams
- [ ] **Owns decisions** regarding component/feature/system-level quality, reliability, efficiency, security, and performance
- [ ] **Balances investments** across quality, reliability, efficiency, security, and performance
- [ ] Reuses and extends solutions where possible; improves products to facilitate easier development
  - **Evidence**:
  - **Gap**:

> **The shift**: L4 = writing good code and following patterns. L5 = **owning technical decisions** at the component/system level — you decide the right tradeoffs. You also start **reducing complexity** and thinking about how your choices affect other teams.

---

## 3. Decision Making and Communication

> *L4 reference: Collaborative approach to engineering (gets input, participates in code reviews). Maps requirements to solutions. Prioritizes multiple tasks with limited guidance. Communicates status/impact/progress to team.*

- [ ] Applies **engineering best practices**: peer review, metrics, architecture design, farming for dissent, collecting stakeholder input, weighing tradeoffs
- [ ] Demonstrates good judgment through **best practices**, not just collaboration
- [ ] **Proactively describes** complex technical issues and their impact to stakeholders
- [ ] Communication is **proactive**, not just responsive
  - **Evidence**:
  - **Gap**:

> **The shift**: L4 = collaborative, gets input and participates. L5 = **driving best practices** (architecture design, farming for dissent, metrics-driven decisions) and **proactively communicating** technical issues to stakeholders before they ask.

---

## 4. Influence and Collaboration

> *L4 reference: Helps support interns and team members. Collaborates effectively with immediate team peers and cross-functional project teams.*

- [ ] **Mentors, develops, and grows** members of my team
- [ ] **Influences and directly guides** new or existing solutions within my scope
- [ ] **Effectively collaborates cross-functionally**: shares/receives cross-team feedback, navigates dissent on my approach
- [ ] Extends beyond immediate team needs on a **project-level basis**
  - **Evidence**:
  - **Gap**:

> **The shift**: L4 = supports others and collaborates within the team. L5 = **actively mentors and grows** people, **collaborates cross-functionally**, and can navigate disagreement — not just participate but influence outcomes.

---

## 5. Living Netflix Values

> *L4 reference: Holds self and team accountable for core values. Speaks up and is comfortable disagreeing with team or manager publicly.*

- [ ] **Embodies** Netflix values and behaviors in everything I do
- [ ] Holds colleagues **on my team and other teams** to the same standard
- [ ] Creates environments where people of **different backgrounds and experiences** feel valued and encouraged to contribute
- [ ] **Elevates voices** other than my own
  - **Evidence**:
  - **Gap**:

> **The shift**: L4 = holds your own team accountable. L5 = extends that **across teams** and actively creates inclusive environments. You become a values role model beyond your immediate team.
